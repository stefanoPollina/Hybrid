CONTINUUM RACK

Knobs — drag up/down. Shift = fine. Double-click = default. Arrow keys when focused.

Cables — drag from an OUT jack to an IN jack. Pull a plug off an input to move or remove it. Right-click a jack to clear it.

Rack — Ctrl/⌘ + scroll (or pinch) to zoom. Drag empty rails or blank panel to pan.

Keys — A W S E D F T G Y H U J K play notes.
